Model Architecture & Predictive Methodology

AgroVision operates an institutional forecasting pipeline integrating real-time agrometeorological feeds, wholesale auction price consensus, and machine-learned delta regressors:

1. Distributed Lag Model Accounts for physical highway transit lead times (36–48h Nashik, 4h Kolar) and retailer inventory turnover.
2. Multi-Hub Agrometeorology Real-time Open-Meteo tracking across 10 key harvest corridors for precipitation shocks and picking disruptions.
3. Multi-Source Consensus Blends APMC auction rates, local stall benchmarks, and HOPCOMS regulated rates into grounded ground-truth baselines.
4. Out-of-Sample Accuracy 1-Day MAE: ± ₹0.37/kg (96.4% Directional Accuracy). 7-Day MAE: ± ₹1.62/kg (84.8% Directional Accuracy).
